Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q1: What does your deep cleaning service include?

    A: Our deep cleaning service encompasses thorough cleaning of all rooms, including floors, walls, windows, and fixtures. We also focus on sanitizing kitchens and bathrooms, ensuring all surfaces are spotless and hygienic.​

  • Q2: How long does a deep cleaning session take?

    A: The duration depends on the size and condition of the space. Typically, a standard home may take between 4 to 8 hours.​

  • Q3: Are your cleaning products safe for children and pets?

    A: Yes, we use eco-friendly and non-toxic cleaning agents that are safe for both children and pets.​

  • Q4: Do I need to be present during the cleaning process?

    A: It's not necessary for you to be present. However, we recommend someone be available at the beginning and end to address any specific concerns and review the completed work.

  • Q5: How often should I schedule a deep cleaning?

    A: For optimal hygiene, we recommend scheduling a deep cleaning every 3 to 6 months, depending on usage and occupancy.
