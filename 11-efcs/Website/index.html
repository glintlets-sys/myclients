FAQ

  • How often should I polish my marble floors?

    We recommend polishing every 1-2 years for residential floors and more frequently for high-traffic areas.

  • Is your process safe for pets and children?

    Yes, we use eco-friendly, non-toxic solutions that are safe for everyone in your home or business.

  • Can you remove deep scratches and stains?

    Our polishing and restoration services can address most scratches and stains, restoring your floor’s natural shine.

  • How much does floor polishing cost in Bangalore?

    Pricing depends on floor type, condition, and area. Contact us for a free on-site quote.

  • How long does the polishing process take?

    A typical 1 BHK takes 4–6 hours. Larger properties are quoted on-site.

  • Do I need to move furniture before the team arrives?

    Our team handles light furniture movement. Heavy items like beds and wardrobes should be cleared in advance.

  • Which areas in Bangalore do you serve?

    We cover all major areas including Whitefield, Koramangala, Indiranagar, Jayanagar, and HSR Layout.

  • Do you provide service on weekends?

    Yes, we are available Monday to Saturday. Sunday visits are by appointment.

  • What payment methods do you accept?

    Cash, UPI, bank transfer, and major credit/debit cards.

  • Do you offer annual maintenance contracts for commercial clients?

    Yes, we offer monthly and quarterly contracts for offices, hotels, and retail spaces. Contact us for a custom quote.
