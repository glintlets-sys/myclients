Frequently Asked Questions

  • How long does an epoxy floor last?

    A: Epoxy flooring is highly durable and can last 10-20 years, depending on the level of traffic, maintenance, and environmental conditions. Regular epoxy floor maintenance can further extend its lifespan.

  • Can epoxy flooring be applied over existing floors?

    A: Yes! Epoxy coatings can be applied over concrete, tiles, and some existing coatings, provided the surface is well-prepared. Our team ensures proper surface grinding and cleaning for a seamless application.

  • Is epoxy flooring suitable for residential use?

    A: Absolutely! While industrial epoxy flooring in Bangalore is common, epoxy is also great for homes, garages, and commercial spaces, offering a sleek, easy-to-clean, and durable finish.

  • How long does epoxy floor installation take?

    A: The installation timeline varies based on floor size and condition, but a standard epoxy floor coating job typically takes 2-4 days, including curing time.

  • How do I maintain my epoxy flooring?

    A: Regular sweeping, gentle mopping with mild cleaners, and avoiding harsh chemicals or abrasive tools can keep your epoxy floors looking new for years. Our epoxy floor maintenance services can also help extend its lifespan.
