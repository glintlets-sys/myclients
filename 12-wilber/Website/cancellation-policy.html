Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 18 June 2026

This Cancellation & Refund Policy explains how order cancellations and refunds are handled by Wilber Impex Pvt Ltd ("Wilber Impex", "we", "us" or "our") for orders placed through our website wilber.co.in and our mobile application.

1. Order Cancellation

You may request cancellation of an order before it has been processed for dispatch. Once an order has been dispatched, it can no longer be cancelled, but it may be eligible for return under the conditions below.

  • To cancel an order, email us at support@wilber.co.in with your order number and registered contact details as early as possible.
  • We will confirm whether the cancellation is successful based on the order's processing status.

2. Custom, Bulk and Made-to-Order Items

Certain products — including custom-formulated, made-to-order, or bulk/industrial quantities — may not be eligible for cancellation once production or packing has begun. Any such restriction will be communicated at the time of order confirmation.

3. Refunds

Where a cancellation or eligible return is approved, refunds will be processed to the original payment method.

  • Approved refunds are typically initiated within 7 business days of confirmation.
  • The time for the amount to reflect in your account depends on your bank or payment provider.
  • Any applicable transaction, shipping, or handling charges may be deducted where stated at the time of purchase.

4. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Items

If you receive a product that is damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, please notify us at support@wilber.co.in within 48 hours of delivery, along with your order number and supporting photographs. After verification, we will arrange a replacement or refund as appropriate.

5. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Products returned in a used, damaged, or altered condition not attributable to us;
  • Requests made beyond the timelines stated in this policy;
  • Items explicitly marked as non-returnable or non-cancellable at the time of purchase.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cancellation & Refund Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

7. Contact Us

For any cancellation, refund, or order-related queries, please contact:

Wilber Impex Pvt Ltd
Email: support@wilber.co.in
Website: wilber.co.in
